Lettuce Yield Predictor

Computer-vision extraction of projected leaf area from 60-second top-down clips, with in-browser regression to predict final yield.

1) Upload video

Fixed assumptions: top-down view, consistent lighting/background, lettuce is the dominant green object.

2) Train (optional but recommended)

Upload your historical trials CSV to fit two linear models: (a) final_area from video features, (b) final_mass from video features.

Required columns in training CSV: trial_id, final_area_cm2, final_mass_g, plus feature columns produced by this app (see “Export features” below).
Model status: not fit (will use default coefficients).
